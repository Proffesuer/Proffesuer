### Hi, I'm Levis Chisira 👋

**Founder & CEO, [Xiracom Limited](https://xiracom.co.ke)** · Full-stack engineer · Eldoret, Kenya

I build education technology designed for East African realities: intermittent networks, in-person classrooms, and learners who already live on their phones. I founded Xiracom (originally Bennavi Software Solutions) in 2022 while studying, and today I lead a team building learning and school-management platforms used by schools and colleges in Kenya.

🎓 BSc Computer Science, The Catholic University of Eastern Africa (CUEA), 2023

---

#### 🚀 What I'm building

- **[XiHub](https://xihub.co.ke)**: an offline-first learning platform that turns class WhatsApp-style groups into learning channels with assignments, live classes, materials and results
- **XEDU**: a school management platform running in multiple Kenyan schools, with M-Pesa (Daraja) payments
- **TimetablerX**: automated timetabling for institutions
- **Xiracom Meet**: live classes and video conferencing
- **XiCast**: multi-tenant in-shop TV advertising with an Android TV app

More of our work: **[github.com/Xiracom-Limited](https://github.com/Xiracom-Limited)**
